= ADR-0034: Naming of edges generated by derived features (Welle N2)
:toc:

== Status

Accepted, implemented (kSTEP Welle N2). Extends ADR-0033 (persistent naming of generated edges); follow-up of its
"Not covered" item "Welle O".

== Context

ADR-0033 names the edges a SWEEP generates (extrude, revolve) from the profile definition. Edges produced by
derived features had no name: the rim of a hole, the section edges of a union or cut, the boundary of a fillet.
After a parameter change (plate width 40 to 60) a geometric selector can hit other edges, and the edge has no
earlier geometry to select on. The question was what OCCT's own history (`Generated()`) really reports for these
operations, so a spike came before any code.

== Spike result (OCCT 7.9.3)

Standalone program `scripts/interop/spike_derived_history.cpp`, run by `scripts/interop/verify-derived-naming.sh
--spike` (output in `build/interop/derived-naming/spike.txt`, nothing is written next to the scripts). All booleans
with `SetRunParallel(false)`; results measured before and after `SimplifyResult()` and after unwrapping the single
solid. `OcctDerivedNamingTest` pins the behaviour as a regression guard (verdicts, not exact indices).

Gates and the resulting decisions:

* *G1:* `Generated(edge)` of fillet and chamfer returns faces that are in the result. `BlendedEdges` and
  `OperandEdges` are built, with role `BOUNDARY`.
* *G2:* `Generated(face)` of booleans returns edges after `SimplifyResult`. `OperandFaces`, `BooleanSection` and
  `HoleWall` are built, with role `SECTION`. The planned fallback (history of `ShapeUpgrade_UnifySameDomain`) was
  not needed.
* *G3:* `Generated(edge)` of a boolean returns vertices, never edges. `OperandEdges` on a boolean or hole is not
  offered; the planner rejects it.

== Decision

=== New sources and roles

`DerivedGenerationSource` is a sealed sub-interface of `GenerationSource`; its members name the INPUT of the
operation, never its geometry, and are resolved against the operands:

[cols="2,3,3"]
|===
| Source | Meaning | Valid on

| `BlendedEdges`
| every edge a fillet or chamfer acted on
| fillet, chamfer

| `OperandEdges(operand = 0, selector)`
| the edges `selector` picks on the filleted / chamfered shape
| fillet, chamfer

| `BooleanSection`
| all faces of both operands, i.e. every section edge
| boolean, hole

| `OperandFaces(operand, faceSelector)`
| the faces the selector picks on operand 0 (left / base) or 1 (right / the drill)
| boolean, hole

| `HoleWall`
| the one cylindrical face of the drill
| hole
|===

`GeneratedRole` gained four values (source-incompatible for exhaustive `when` in third-party code, noted in the
CHANGELOG):

* `BOUNDARY` (fillet, chamfer): the edges of the blend face(s) the source produced that border a face OUTSIDE all
  faces the operation generated. Seam and degenerated edges are excluded. For one rounded vertical edge of a box
  these are the two spring lines and the two arcs (4 edges); for a rounded hole rim the two circles. A split into
  spring lines and cross-section edges is a possible follow-up.
* `SECTION` (boolean, hole): the edges `Generated()` reports for the faces named by the source, without seam and
  degenerated edges, measured after `SimplifyResult()` and unwrapping the solid.
* `RIM_ENTRY` / `RIM_EXIT` (hole, source exactly `[HoleWall]`): the SECTION edges of the hole wall are grouped into
  rings (connected components over end points that coincide within 1e-6) and ordered by their mean distance from the
  hole's start point ALONG THE DRILL DIRECTION, not along a world axis. The first ring is the entry; the last is the
  exit and exists only if there are at least two rings. A blind hole therefore has an entry and no exit (spike S4),
  which makes `RIM_EXIT` an `EdgeNamingException` instead of a guess. Two rings closer than 1e-6 along the drill are
  ambiguous and also an `EdgeNamingException`. A hole that breaks out through a side face merges entry, side and exit
  into one outline; it is detected by a straight section edge, by a component whose extent along the drill exceeds
  the drill diameter, and by the faces: every component must lie on exactly one non-wall face; a plane face must have
  its normal at most 45 degrees off the drill (a ring on a more inclined entry face is rejected), and a curved face is
  accepted only when at least two rings lie on that same face, because one closed outline on a curved face cannot be told from a
  side breakout (a grazing or large drill through a rod). Then `RIM_ENTRY` and `RIM_EXIT` raise an
  `EdgeNamingException` while `SECTION` stays usable as the fallback. Consequence: a blind hole into a curved face has
  no usable `RIM_ENTRY`; use `SECTION`. At most 4096 edges are classified.

=== Structural validation before any native call

`TrackingPlanner` classifies the generator (`SWEEP`, `BLEND`, `BOOLEAN`, `HOLE`) and checks the matrix: SIDE / START /
END only on sweeps with profile sources; BOUNDARY on fillet / chamfer with `BlendedEdges` or `OperandEdges(0)`;
SECTION on booleans and holes with `OperandFaces(0|1)`, `BooleanSection` (and `HoleWall` on holes); RIM roles on holes
with exactly `[HoleWall]`. Mixed profile and derived sources, `OperandEdges` on a boolean or hole (gate G3),
`HoleWall` on anything but a hole, an operand outside 0..1, duplicates, more than 1024 sources and selector trees
beyond the depth or node limit are `IllegalArgumentException`s. `FeatureHistory` runs the same check on every
state, so replacing a boolean by a translate while a name still refers to it is refused. Derived sources contain
operand slots and selectors, never feature indices, so `FeatureIndexRemap` (add / remove / move) needs no change; the
`featureIndex` of `GeneratedEdges` is remapped as before.

=== Resolution

`FeatureRebuilder` builds a derived generator that some consumer names with `buildDerivedNamed`, which sets BOTH the
generation map and the edge history of that feature (it can be a generator and lie on the tracking path of another
consumer at the same time). `GenerationMap.images` answers derived sources by role; everything else
(`within`, `expected`, following the edges through later operations, the structured error with candidates) is the
code path of ADR-0033.

=== No migration

kSTEP has no database and does not persist the history (ADR-0033), so there is nothing to migrate.

== Native bridge

Three new entry points: `nativeFilletEdgesNamed`, `nativeChamferEdgesNamed`, `nativeBooleanOpNamed`. Each takes the
operation's arguments plus `queries`, an `IntArray` of `(slot, kind, index)` triples (slot 0 = the shape, or 0 / 1 =
left / right of a boolean; kind 0 = edge, 1 = face; index = position in `TopExp::MapShapes` order of that operand), and
two out holders: `outHistory` (as in the `Tracked` variants) and `outMap`. The map is
`[queryCount, (nEdges, idx..., nFaces, idx...)*]` over the edge and face maps of the result, at most 2 000 000 ints;
Kotlin decodes and validates it with `decodeDerivedGeneration`.

History and map come from the SAME maker, inside the same critical section. The existing implementations
(`filletEdgesImpl`, `chamferEdgesImpl`, `booleanOpImpl`) gained optional parameters; the plain and tracked entries pass
`nullptr`, so named and plain builds share one construction path and have identical edge and face indices (a test
compares them).

* *Lock order.* Unlike the fresh sweeps of ADR-0033, a fillet, chamfer or boolean result shares TShapes with the
  registered operands, so `Generated()` and the index lookups run UNDER `g_mutex`: lookup, range check of the
  queries, `Build()`, solid checks, `encodeEdgeHistory`, `encodeDerivedGeneration`; then unlock; then `throwJava`;
  then `publishHistory(outHistory)` and `publishHistory(outMap)`; then `registerShape`. Nothing is registered on an
  error path and the full catch cascade is unchanged.
* *Validation.* Before the lock: queries non-null, length divisible by 3, 1..4096 triples, slot below the operand
  count, kind in {0, 1}, index non-negative, both holders with a slot. Under the lock and before `Build()`: every
  index below the operand's extent, else `IllegalArgumentException` and no work done.
* *APIs.* `BRepBuilderAPI_MakeShape::Generated`, virtual and overridden in `BRepFilletAPI_MakeFillet`,
  `BRepFilletAPI_MakeChamfer` and `BRepAlgoAPI_BooleanOperation`; all exist in OCCT 7.9. *No new toolkit*
  (TKFillet, TKBO and TKBool are linked already). OCCT 7.8 is not tested and not claimed.

== Security

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Area | Measure
| DoS | At most 1024 sources and 4096 `Generated()` queries per call, in Kotlin and natively; the map payload is
capped at 2 000 000 ints; rim classification at 4096 edges; the selector depth and node limits apply to
`OperandEdges`; the existing face limits of fillet, chamfer and boolean stay; everything is validated before the
native call.
| Hostile input | `decodeDerivedGeneration` validates every count and index (`DerivedGenerationDecodeTest`); the natives
validate the queries independently (`OcctDerivedNamingTest` feeds them malformed triples and checks that no shape was
registered).
| Resource leaks | `DerivedNamedShape` is `AutoCloseable` and closed if decoding or classification fails; the drill of
a hole is closed in `use {}` on every path; `FeatureRebuilder` closes all built shapes on any error; a test measures the
native live-shape count over 100 failing builds (with slack, because the Cleaner works asynchronously).
| Concurrency | All registry access under `g_mutex`; no JNI call that can run Java code under the lock.
| Logging | Messages contain indices, source descriptions (at most 64 characters) and at most 32 candidates.
| Crypto, SSRF, licence | Not relevant; no new dependency.
|===

== Not covered / follow-up

* Vertices and faces as the TARGET of a name; `Generated(vertex)` (corner blends) is empty for the cases measured
  (S8) and not built.
* Naming across edits that change the source topology; a persisted history (ADR-0033).
* Multi-body generators; `TranslateFeature` as a generator (name the feature before the translate).
* `OperandEdges` on booleans (gate G3: OCCT reports vertices).
* Sub-kinds of `BOUNDARY` (spring lines versus cross-section edges).
* A `RIM_*` classification for a drill that does not cross a planar face perpendicularly is ordered by ring
  position along the drill; the geometry of an oblique rim is not interpreted further.
* OCCT 7.8.

== Consequences

* A hole, union or fillet can be dimensioned by sketch and named by what it was given; a parameter change keeps the
  same logical edges, and every failure is a structured error.
* `GeneratedRole` gained values: exhaustive `when` expressions in third-party code need new branches (pre-1.0).
* The spike pins OCCT's behaviour: if a later OCCT version stops reporting `Generated()` after `SimplifyResult()`, the
  regression guard fails instead of the names silently resolving to nothing.
