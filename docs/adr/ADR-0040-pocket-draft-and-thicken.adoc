= ADR-0040: Pocket and hole from a face, draft and thicken (Welle Q2)
:toc:

== Status

Accepted, implemented (kSTEP Welle Q2). Builds on ADR-0037 (face selection, shell), ADR-0034 (edge history) and ADR-0036
(shape operations). Every number below comes from `scripts/interop/spike_face_ops_history.cpp` (OCCT 7.9.3, macOS arm64).

== Context

ADR-0037 listed three operations as "not in this wave": a pocket or hole starting on a chosen face, a draft, and thickening
a face. All three take a face (or faces) of an existing solid as their input. The spike measured what OCCT does before any
code was written; the decisions below follow the measurements, and what the measurements did not support is not built.

== Decisions

P1:: *A pocket is a prism cut.* The profile becomes a planar face, `BRepPrimAPI_MakePrism` sweeps it into the body along
the negative face normal, `BRepAlgoAPI_Cut` subtracts it. That is the same path as `cut` and `hole`. `BRepFeat_MakePrism`
(toolkit `TKFeat`, not linked) is NOT used, so the link line and the CI packages are unchanged. The tool starts a margin
`max(1e-3, 1e-3 * bounding-box diagonal)` OUTSIDE the face, so it never starts coplanar with the face it cuts; the spike
shows identical results for a flush and an overshooting start (23500, 11 faces), the overshoot is simply the numerically
safer choice. A through pocket is `extent + 2 * margin` long, where `extent` is the spread of the bounding-box corners
projected on the face normal.

P2:: *Face-local coordinate system.* Origin = the centroid of the target face, `N` = the outward normal, `U` = global +X
projected into the face plane and normalised (global +Y when `|N . X| > 1 - 1e-9`), `V = N x U`. Profile coordinates are
`(u, v)` relative to the origin. Measured: top face of the box (`N = +Z`): `U = +X`, `V = +Y`; face with `N = +X`: `U = +Y`,
`V = +Z`. A rectangle has no rotation in Q2 (use a polygon).

P3:: *The profile must lie strictly inside the face; nothing is clipped.* Every polygon vertex, every edge midpoint (a circle:
its centre and 32 contour points) is classified with `BRepClass_FaceClassifier` (tolerance 1e-7) and must be `TopAbs_IN`;
in addition the area of `Common(profile face, target face)` must equal the profile area to 1e-6 relative, which also
catches an edge that crosses a concave rim. Measured: a point exactly on the rim is `ON` (also at an offset of 1e-9), a
point beyond it is `OUT`. OCCT itself accepts a profile that crosses the rim and cuts a notch (50 x 10 on the 40 x 30 face
gives V = 22000, 10 faces, a quite different shape), so without this check a typo would silently produce a valid but wrong
body. An open pocket (a slot reaching an edge) is a follow-up.

P4:: *Depth.* `PocketDepth.Blind(d)` needs `0 < d < extent`; `d >= extent` is refused with "use through = true" (a blind
pocket that is as deep as the body is a through cut and would hide that). A blind depth that reaches through the material
under the profile (removed volume < profile area x depth, e.g. on a stepped body, or when it breaks into an existing
cavity under the profile) is refused with "blind depth reaches through the material under the profile".
`PocketDepth.Through` cuts the whole body.

P5:: *One face, no guessing.* `OcctKernel.pocket(shape, FaceSelector, ...)` and a `PocketFeature` with a `SelectFaces`
reference resolve with `expected = 1` even when the feature says `expected = null`; a tie is a `FaceSelectionException`
with the candidate list (`KSTEP-S-011`). `FaceIndices` must hold exactly one index. Only planar faces; the result must be
one solid with one shell (a pocket that splits the body or leaves an internal void is refused, like `boolean`).

P6:: *Edge history through a pocket.* The cut keeps every edge of the original solid by identity as long as the profile is
inside the face (measured: 12 of 12 edges kept for the rectangle, the circle and the through hole; the 200 and 400 face
prisms: 594 of 594 and 1194 of 1194). `pocketWithHistory` therefore returns the standard `EdgeHistory`, and a
`TrackedEdges` reference follows an edge through a pocket. The new pocket walls and floor have no persistent names yet
(`GeneratorKind.NONE`, see "Not in this wave").

D1:: *Draft sign.* `angleDegrees > 0` means: the material NARROWS along the pull direction of the neutral plane (the drafted
faces lean into the material), `< 0` widens it. Measured with `BRepOffsetAPI_DraftAngle::Add(F, pull, angle, plane)`:
pull +Z, neutral plane z = 0, four side faces of the 40 x 30 x 20 box, `+5 deg` gives V = 21631.963 (top face smaller);
`-5 deg` gives V = 26531.328. The same body results from pull -Z with the neutral plane z = 20 and `+5 deg`. The public
sign is therefore the OCCT sign and no inversion is needed; the bridge nevertheless checks it: when every drafted face lies
on the pull side of the neutral plane the volume must shrink for a positive and grow for a negative angle, and when it lies
on the other side the reverse. If the faces straddle the plane only "V > 0 and V changed" is required (measured: neutral
plane z = 10, +5 deg gives V = 24020.411, the upper half narrows and the lower half widens).

D2:: *Draft preconditions and failures.* `1e-3 <= |angle| < 45` degrees, 1 to 64 planar faces, a neutral plane
(`NeutralPlane(origin, pullDirection)`; the pull direction is normalised, `Direction3` already forbids the zero vector).
`AddDone()` is false for a face parallel to the neutral plane (measured: `Draft_ErrorStatus` = `FaceRecomputation`, 1) and
the bridge reports status name and the problematic face index. `Build()` is false when the angle is too large for the
geometry (measured: a 10 x 10 x 20 box at 30 and 44 degrees; the top would degenerate). Both are `OcctGeometryException`
(`KSTEP-S-003`). Not-planar faces are refused (`IllegalArgumentException`).

D3:: *The volume of a drafted box is the prismatoid volume, not the frustum volume.* The cross sections of the box (40 x 30
to 36.5 x 26.5) are not similar rectangles, so `h/3 (A1 + A2 + sqrt(A1 A2))` is only an approximation: 21631.016 against
the exact `h/6 (A1 + 4 Am + A2) = 24000 - 28000 tan a + 32000/3 tan^2 a = 21631.963` (difference 0.95). The tests use the
exact formula.

D4:: *No edge history through a draft.* Measured: all 12 edges of the drafted box are reported neither `Modified` nor kept
(12 "lost"), at the face level 2 of 6 faces are `Modified` and 0 survive by identity. A draft therefore rebuilds every edge;
`DraftFeature` is not trackable and a `TrackedEdges` reference through it fails with this reason instead of guessing.

T1:: *Thicken = per-face outward offset, built.* `BRepOffset_MakeOffset::Initialize(S, 0, Confusion, Skin, Intersection =
true, SelfInter = false, GeomAbs_Intersection, Thickening = false)` plus `SetOffsetOnFace(F, d)` for each chosen face
(the other faces keep offset 0) moves the face by `d` and extends its neighbours. Measured on the box: top +2 gives
V = 26400, box z 0..22, 6 faces, valid, identical in three repeats; top +2 and +X +3 gives 28380 (a 43 x 30 x 22 block); top
and +X both +2 give 27720 (42 x 30 x 22). `BRepOffset_MakeOffset` is public in 7.9.3 and its symbols are in `libTKOffset`
(already linked, no new `-l`). `thicken(shape, faces, d)` takes `d > 0` (outward); a negative offset works (top -2 gives
21600) but is a different operation ("push in") and is not offered. Planar faces only; sanity bound
`0 < V1 - V0 <= d * (total surface area) * (1 + 1e-3)`. No edge history: the algorithm rebuilds every face (0 of 6 faces
survive by identity).

T2:: *Thickening a single sheet face into a solid is NOT built.* `BRepOffset_MakeOffset` with `Thickening = true` on an
isolated face does produce a valid solid (the 40 x 30 face with d = +-2 gives V = 2400, 6 faces, on the side of the sign), but
kSTEP's operations take exactly one solid, a face is not a shape here, and a sheet shape would have to flow through the
whole shape / feature / export pipeline. That is a different wave.

T3:: *"Pad from a face" (extrude the face and fuse) is NOT offered under the name thicken.* It gives the same volume on the
box (26400, but 10 faces) because it does not extend the neighbours; calling it thicken would be a lie. It is a `boolean`
of an extrusion today.

L1:: *Limits, measured.* Pocket in a 200-face prism 35 ms, in a 400-face prism 73 ms -> `MAX_POCKET_INPUT_FACES = 400`,
1024 profile points. Offset of one face of a 200-face prism 129 ms, of a 400-face prism 407 ms ->
`MAX_THICKEN_INPUT_FACES = 200`, 64 faces. Draft of 12 / 24 / 48 faces 8 / 16 / 33 ms -> `MAX_DRAFT_INPUT_FACES = 200`, 64
faces per call (64 faces of a 198-gon fail geometrically, because the neighbouring faces are 1.8 degrees apart: a reported
`IsDone() == false`, not a count limit). Lengths in `[1e-7, 1e7]`, coordinates within +/-1e7.

L2:: *Error codes.* No new code. `FaceSelectionException` is `KSTEP-S-011`, an OCCT failure is `KSTEP-S-003`, invalid
arguments follow the existing mapping.

== Sign conventions

* Draft: positive narrows the material along `NeutralPlane.pullDirection`; the faces keep their size on the neutral plane.
* Thicken: the distance is positive and moves the face along its OUTWARD normal.
* Pocket: the profile is cut along the INWARD normal of the face.

== Face-local coordinate system

See P2. Pocket profile coordinates are relative to the centroid of the face AS IT IS NOW: after a pocket the centroid of a
face with an inner wire moves slightly, so a second pocket on the same face uses the new centroid.

== Failure modes

Argument errors (`IllegalArgumentException`) are raised before the native bridge is needed: non-finite or out-of-range
numbers, a polygon with fewer than 3 or more than 1024 points, coincident consecutive points, no area, self-intersection,
an empty, duplicate or too long face list. Geometry errors that need the shape are raised natively under the lock:
non-planar face, face index out of range, profile not inside the face, blind depth not less than the body extent. OCCT
failures (`IllegalStateException` in the bridge, `OcctGeometryException` in Kotlin): `IsDone()` false, more than one solid
or an internal void, an invalid result, an implausible volume change, a draft that goes the wrong way.

== Not in this wave

* Names for the pocket walls / floor and the drafted or offset faces (`GeneratedFaces`, `TrackedFaces`): they are new faces
  and need an `encodeFaceHistory`; until then `GeneratedEdges` through these features is refused with a pointer here.
* Rotating a pocket rectangle; pockets on curved faces; a pocket across several faces; open pockets and slots that reach
  the rim (P3).
* Draft of non-planar faces (the cylinder case works for a pull parallel to the axis but is not generalised), variable
  draft angles, a draft with a parting line.
* Edge tracking through a draft or thicken (D4, T1).
* Negative thickening ("push in") and thickening a sheet face into a solid (T2); "pad from face" as a feature (T3).
* A migration: kSTEP persists no feature history, so there is none. Files that use none of the new features are unchanged
  (checked byte for byte against the previous commit, see Verification).

== Verification

Hand calculations: box 40 x 30 x 20 (V 24000). Pocket 10 x 10 x 5: 24000 - 500 = 23500, 11 faces (6 + 4 walls + floor).
Hole d 8, depth 6: 24000 - 96 pi = 23698.4071, 8 faces; through: 24000 - 320 pi = 22994.6904, 7 faces. Rebuilt with height
30: 40 * 30 * 30 - 500 = 35500. Draft 5 deg, exact: with `t = tan 5 deg`, `24000 - 28000 t + 32000/3 t^2 = 21631.9629`,
outward `+28000 t`: 26531.3281. Thicken top by 2: 40 * 30 * 22 = 26400.

`scripts/interop/verify-face-ops-2.sh` exports the six example scripts and compares them with these numbers through OCCT's
typed reader and FreeCAD; `--baseline` additionally exports the pre-Q2 scripts with the CLI of the previous commit and with
this tree and compares them byte for byte (time stamp normalised). Tests: `FaceOpsValidationTest` (no OCCT),
`OcctPocketFeatureTest`, `OcctDraftFeatureTest`, `OcctThickenFeatureTest` and the extended
`CliExportOcctFaceOpsIntegrationTest`. The decisive evidence is the CI job "OCCT + PlaneGCS required" on Ubuntu and macOS.
